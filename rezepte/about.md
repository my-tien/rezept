# The German Bakery

Welcome to the bakery!

The smell of freshly baked bread hangs in the air and the walls are lined with bread and pastries.

Opening hours:
Every day
07:00 to 01:00

A note on the wall kindly asks you not to touch any goods if you don’t intend to eat them.
